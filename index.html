How to use

Sub Arc Calc helps you line up subwoofers so they work as an arc. Enter your boxes and the room, and it gives you the spacing between boxes, the radius of the arc, and the delay each box needs.

  1. Enter your setup. Number of boxes, box width, total line length and arc angle.
  2. Add the air. Temperature and humidity change the speed of sound, so the delays stay accurate.
  3. Optional margin. Edge margin keeps the ends of the line free, so the boxes sit closer together.
  4. Read the result. The big circle shows the box spacing. The small circles show radius and centre-to-centre length. The drawing previews the arc.
  5. Set the delays. In the table, type each box's Norm. delay into your processor. The box with the smallest delay reads 0.
  6. Save it. Add a venue name, then press Save results. Load, copy or delete it later under Saved setups.

Touch and hold the little fader at the right of a number for a moment, then drag up or down to change it. A quick swipe just scrolls the page. Tap any small i or underlined column name to see what it means.
